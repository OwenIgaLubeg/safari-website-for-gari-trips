Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to the most common questions about planning your Uganda safari.

Still Have Questions?

Our team is available 7 days a week to help you plan your perfect adventure.

How quickly will you respond to my inquiry?

We guarantee a response within 24 hours to all inquiries received via our contact form, email, or WhatsApp. For urgent matters, please call us directly at +256 704 018 220.

Can I customize my safari itinerary?

Absolutely! Every safari we create is tailor-made. We work with you to design an itinerary that matches your interests, budget, travel style, and schedule. Whether you want luxury lodges or budget-friendly options, we've got you covered.

What is included in your safari packages?

Our packages typically include 4x4 safari vehicle, professional English-speaking guide, accommodation, meals as per itinerary, park entrance fees, and airport transfers. Specific inclusions vary by package — we'll provide a detailed breakdown in your quote.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 3-6 months in advance, especially for gorilla trekking permits which are limited and in high demand. However, we can also accommodate last-minute bookings depending on availability.

Do you offer airport pickup services?

Yes! We provide complimentary airport pickup and drop-off at Entebbe International Airport for all our safari clients. Our driver will be waiting for you with a welcome sign bearing your name.

Is it safe to travel in Uganda?

Uganda is one of the safest countries in East Africa for tourists. Our experienced guides ensure your safety throughout the journey, and we maintain the highest safety standards on all our tours. We also provide comprehensive travel advice before your trip.
