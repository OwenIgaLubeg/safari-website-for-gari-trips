The Remarkable Return

Bringing the Rhino Back to Uganda

In the 1980s, rampant poaching drove the rhino population in Uganda to complete extinction in the wild. For decades, the iconic creature was absent from the Pearl of Africa's landscapes.

However, a remarkable conservation story began in 2005 with the establishment of the Ziwa Rhino and Wildlife Ranch. Located in the Nakasongola district along the Kampala-Gulu highway, this 70-square-kilometer sanctuary was created with a singular, ambitious goal: to reintroduce rhinos to Uganda and eventually release them into protected national parks.

"Today, thanks to the tireless efforts of Rhino Fund Uganda, Ziwa is home to over 30 Southern White Rhinos, making it the only place in the country where you can track these magnificent giants on foot."

What to Expect

The Tracking Experience

Rhino tracking at Ziwa is a unique, intimate, and highly regulated experience designed for both your safety and the well-being of the animals.

On-Foot Adventure

Unlike traditional vehicle-based game drives, rhino tracking is done entirely on foot. Accompanied by an experienced, armed ranger, you will quietly navigate the savannah and woodland trails to locate the rhinos.

Duration & Timing

Treks typically last 1 to 2 hours, depending on the rhinos' location. The best times to track are early morning (7:00 AM) or late afternoon (4:00 PM) when the animals are most active and the light is perfect for photography.

Small Groups

To minimize disturbance to the wildlife, tracking groups are strictly limited to a maximum of 6 people per ranger. This ensures an intimate, personalized, and low-impact encounter with the rhinos.

The Sanctuary

Ziwa Rhino & Wildlife Ranch

Spanning 70 square kilometers of diverse habitat, Ziwa is more than just a rhino sanctuary. It is a thriving ecosystem that supports a wide variety of birdlife, antelopes, baboons, and reptiles.

The sanctuary operates as a non-profit initiative under the guidance of Rhino Fund Uganda. Every tracking fee paid by visitors goes directly toward anti-poaching patrols, veterinary care, habitat management, and community development programs in the surrounding areas.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your rhino tracking adventure.

Need More Info?

Our travel experts are ready to answer any specific questions about your trip.

Is rhino tracking safe?

Yes, it is very safe. You will be accompanied by a trained, armed Uganda Wildlife Authority (UWA) ranger at all times. The rangers are experts in rhino behavior and will ensure you maintain a safe and respectful distance (typically 7-10 meters) from the animals.

Can children participate in rhino tracking?

Yes! Rhino tracking is a fantastic family-friendly activity. Children aged 5 and above are welcome to participate. The terrain is relatively flat and easy to walk, making it accessible for most fitness levels.

What should I wear and bring?

Wear comfortable, closed-toe walking shoes or light hiking boots, long trousers, and neutral-colored clothing (khaki, green, or brown). Bring a hat, sunscreen, insect repellent, a bottle of water, and your camera or binoculars.

How much does rhino tracking cost?

The standard tracking fee is $50 per person for foreign non-residents, $40 for foreign residents, and 30,000 UGX for East African citizens. This fee includes the guided walk and contributes directly to rhino conservation efforts.

Can I combine this with other activities?

Absolutely. Ziwa is perfectly located on the main highway between Kampala and Murchison Falls National Park. Many of our clients combine rhino tracking with a Shoebill Stork canoe ride at nearby Limbach Swamp, making it an ideal stopover on a northern circuit safari.
