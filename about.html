Welcome to Gari Trips

Discover. Explore. Experience.

Welcome to Gari Trips Uganda, your trusted local partner for unforgettable adventures across Uganda and East Africa. We are a Ugandan-owned tour company dedicated to creating authentic travel experiences that connect visitors with the region's breathtaking landscapes, diverse wildlife, vibrant cultures, and hidden gems.

Whether you dream of trekking endangered mountain gorilla , witnessing the mighty Nile River, exploring vast savannahs teeming with wildlife, or immersing yourself in local communities , we are here to make your journey seamless, memorable, and truly extraordinary.

Immanuel Wamba Founder & Lead Guide
Who We Are

The Pearl of Africa, Unveiled

Gari Trips Uganda was founded with a passion for showcasing the beauty and diversity of Uganda, often referred to as the "Pearl of Africa." With years of experience in tourism, logistics, and customer service, our team understands what it takes to deliver exceptional travel experiences from the moment you inquire until you return home with lasting memories.

As a locally owned and operated company, we provide insider knowledge, personalized service, and genuine hospitality that only a local expert can offer.

We take pride in introducing our guests to the authentic Uganda that we know and love — from the misty mountains of Bwindi to the roaring waters of Murchison Falls.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

Our Vision

To become one of East Africa's most trusted and respected tour operators by delivering exceptional customer experiences, innovative travel solutions, and responsible tourism practices.
