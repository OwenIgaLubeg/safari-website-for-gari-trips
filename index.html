What measures are in place to ensure the safety of my personal information?

We take the security of your personal information seriously. Our website employs state-of-the-art encryption technology to safeguard your data during the booking process. Additionally, we adhere to strict privacy policies and comply with industry standards to protect your information. Uganda Wildlife Authority

Official Permit & Tariff Update

Date: 26 February 2026

Introduction of Low Season Gorilla & Chimpanzee Permit Rates

Uganda Wildlife Authority (UWA) has introduced discounted low season permit rates applicable during the months of April, May and November.

Activity FNR (USD) FR (USD)
Gorilla Tracking $600 $500
Chimpanzee Tracking $200 $150

These rates take immediate effect and are intended to stimulate travel during the low season while maintaining Uganda's exceptional primate tracking experience.

Important Notice

Effective 1 March 2026, the current 7-day reservation window for permits has been suspended.

Going forward, permits will only be secured upon full payment at the time of booking.

Revised Tariffs Effective 1 January 2027

Activity FNR FR
Gorilla Tracking $800 $700
Gorilla Habituation $1,800 $1,600
Chimpanzee Tracking (Kibale) $300 $200
Chimpanzee Habituation $400 $300
Experiential Lion Tracking $500 $300

These changes are part of UWA's continued commitment to sustainable conservation financing and improved visitor experiences.
